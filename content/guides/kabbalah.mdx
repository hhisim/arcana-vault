# Kabbalah

A system of correspondences, not a decorative mystical set. This guide walks the Tree from sefirot to the Four Worlds, and shows where the tradition and the popular version diverge.

**Compiled from 5 essays and roughly 15,823 words of original writing** in the Vault of Arcana archive. Every entry below is a working link into the full essay, not a summary standing in for it.
